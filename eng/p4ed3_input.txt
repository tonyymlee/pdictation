Being a good student does not mean just getting high grades at school. It also means having good character⁴ — doing the right things and being a nice person. 
You cannot change your character overnight, but there are many things you can do to help you become a good person. The easiest way is to start with your school life. You can start by keeping your school desk clean and tidy. Being tidy is better than being messy. It shows that you know how to look after your own things. Your classmates will want to come over to your seat and chat with you! 
Remember to greet your teachers and classmates at school. Don’t be shy or rude. A polite and outgoing student can make a lot of friends! You can also try to be generous. No one likes a selfish person. Next time you have some cake, give the biggest piece to your friends. You will feel happier sharing food with others than eating it on your own. 
Students with these good qualities usually grow up to become good people. It is time for you to set goals for yourself.
